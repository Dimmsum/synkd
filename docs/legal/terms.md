## 1. What whosfree is

whosfree shows you and the people you choose when you're free or busy. It uses schedules you upload, schedules you enter yourself, and (if you connect it) your Google Calendar. You can connect with friends, join groups, send short "pings" to people who are free, and find times when a group is free. ## 6. Friends, groups and pings

- **Friends.** A friend connection needs both people to accept. Each of you chooses what the other can see.
- **Groups.** A group has one **admin**. The admin can give members permissions (for example to invite people, remove members, edit the group, or ping the whole group), and can remove members or delete the group. **Admins don't get extra access to members' schedules.** Groups currently have a limit of [GROUP MEMBER LIMIT] members.
- **Invite links.** Anyone who has an invite link can use it to ask to join, so share links carefully. Admins can revoke or regenerate a link at any time.
- **Offline friends.** You can add someone who isn't on whosfree under a nickname, and upload or enter their schedule. **You must have their permission first**, and you confirm this each time you add one. Only you can see an offline friend: they're never shown to anyone else or merged with a whosfree account. Delete their schedule if they ask you to or withdraw their permission. Offline friends can't be pinged, but you can invite them to whosfree.
- **Pings** are short nudges (up to 140 characters). They're not a full messaging service, and they expire. Pings are limited to prevent spam, and you can mute people, set quiet hours or block anyone.
